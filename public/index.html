Educational Games

Number Haul

A math tug-of-war. Two teams, two devices, one rope. Answer arithmetic problems correctly and quickly to haul the rope to your side.

Team Games

Create your own race or buzzer game for any subject, join with a class code, or put it up on the big screen.

Course Notes

Math, physics, and chemistry notes with properly typeset equations — read them straight, or borrow a problem for a game.
