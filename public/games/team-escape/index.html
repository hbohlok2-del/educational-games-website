Your team is locked in. Solve each room's puzzles to open its door and find one digit of the vault code, then crack the vault before time runs out.

Join the Escape

Big Screen

Project the building for the class. The teacher can open a door for a stuck team from here.

How it works: each room has up to 5 puzzles: padlocks, typed answers, step puzzles, matching pairs and graphs. Solve them all to open the door and reveal one digit of the vault code. A wrong answer sets off a 5-second alarm. A hint costs 30 seconds; a teacher opening a door costs 2 minutes. Enter the full code at the vault to escape. The first team out wins.
