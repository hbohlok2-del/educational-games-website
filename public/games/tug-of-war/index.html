Big Screen

Put the rope and live scores up on a projector for everyone to see.

How scoring works: a correct answer hauls the rope toward your side: 10 points in under 5 seconds, 7 points between 5-10 seconds, 5 points past that. A wrong answer earns nothing and costs you a moment before your next question, so speed only pays off when you're also right. First team to pull the flag past their line wins the haul.
